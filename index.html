Tindahan Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tindahan Tracker handles information when you use the application. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your information secure.

1. Information Stored in the App

Tindahan Tracker allows users to enter and manage information needed for store operations, including:

  • Product names, prices, and categories
  • Inventory and stock records
  • Sales transactions and reports
  • Customer names and debt (utang) records
  • Other store management information entered by the user

2. Data Storage

Information entered into Tindahan Tracker is stored locally on the user's device using a local database. The application does not use an online database or developer-operated cloud database to store this information.

3. Camera and Barcode/QR Scanning

The application may request camera access to scan product barcodes and QR codes. Camera access is used for the scanning function. The app does not intentionally store or transmit camera images or video as part of this function.

4. Data Sharing

Tindahan Tracker does not operate an online service that collects or shares the locally stored store records. Users are responsible for any information they choose to export, back up, or share outside the application.

5. Data Security

Store information is kept on the user's device. Users should protect their devices with appropriate security measures and keep any exported backup files in a safe location.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information remains stored on the device until it is deleted by the user or removed through applicable device or app storage actions. Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored information. Users should save any important records before uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Tindahan Tracker is a store management application. It is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
